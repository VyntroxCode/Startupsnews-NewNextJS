Section 1

What this is, in one page

StartupNews.fyi looks like a news website from the outside. Inside, it is four products sharing one codebase and one database. That is the single most important thing to understand about it — almost every architectural decision follows from it.

1. The public news site

Articles, categories, authors, search, startup events, reports, brand stories and press releases. This is what readers and Google see.

2. The newsroom CMS

Where staff write, schedule and publish everything above — plus banners, partner logos and editable page content.

3. Internal business tools

HR tool, Sales Tracker, Network Manager (CRM), Partnership Tracker, IT helpdesk and the newsletter engine. Staff-only, role-gated.

4. Employee self-service

Staff punch in and out, request leave, and upload KYC and onboarding documents from their own portal.

A fifth piece runs with no screen at all: a background worker that pulls in RSS feeds from other publications, publishes scheduled content the moment it is due, and sends the daily morning email.

Why it matters Because all four live in one app, a change to something shared — login, roles, the database pool, the CSS reset — can affect a completely unrelated part of the product. That is why every change is recorded in arch.md, and why anything medium or larger gets its own version entry in this document.

The one operational rule that came from a real outage A rebuild must always be followed by a process restart, in the same run. On 2026-08-02 the site was rebuilt while the old process kept running; it carried on pointing browsers at JavaScript files that no longer existed, and the admin panel broke in a way no amount of refreshing could fix. deploy.sh exists precisely so that pair can never come apart again.
Standing rule for Jarvis Never build, never restart PM2, and never apply a migration unless explicitly asked. Code changes are written and type-checked; going live is always the owner's call.

That is fixed, and the screens were redesigned around the way Jira lays things out: a board of columns, a card per ticket, a "Create ticket" dialog, and an issue view with the details down the right-hand side.
